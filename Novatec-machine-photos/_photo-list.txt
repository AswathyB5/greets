PLURITANK 1400 - large multi-tank line (photographed at Novatec's works)
  PLURITANK-1400-line-overview.jpg          Complete line from above, with main electrical cabinet
                                            and unloading station. BEST IMAGE IN THE PACK - use this
                                            as the PLURITANK category header.
  PLURITANK-1400-line-and-loading.jpg       Along the line, tanks and roller conveyor, operator in shot
  PLURITANK-1400-ultrasonic-generators.jpg  Generator rack, filtration skids, pumps and air extraction

  The 1400 is a 14-station line: 3 ultrasonic cleaning tanks with basket agitation,
  3 turbulent-flow rinses, DI ultrasonic rinse, cascade DI rinse and a 5-position
  hot air tunnel dryer. Internal tank 700 x 1200 x 1650 mm, basket 540 x 1090 x 1540 mm.
