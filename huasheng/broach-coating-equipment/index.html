Broach coating equipment

Dedicated vertical arc coating equipment specially designed by Huasheng for extra-long cutting tools, such as broaches, long drills, and form cutters. Features an extended vertical working zone, multi-zone heating, and distributed arc cathodes for uniform coating thickness along the entire length.
