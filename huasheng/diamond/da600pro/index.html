DA600PRO HFCVD diamond coating machine

The DA600PRO is Huasheng's advanced hot-filament CVD (HFCVD) diamond coating machine. Equipped with a parallel filament array for highly uniform chamber temperature, it deposits microcrystalline and nanocrystalline diamond coatings (4–30 µm) on cemented carbide inserts, end mills, and drills for high-speed machining of graphite, carbon composites, and high-silicon aluminium alloys.

Enquire about this View on Huasheng website

DA series overview

Hot filament CVD diamond coating with a parallel filament array for uniform temperature. Not related to CVA aluminizing.

Typical applications

Graphite, composites, CFRP, PCB ceramic substrates, AlSi alloys (Si>12%), woodworking.

Key features

  • Deposition up to 1.0 µm/h
  • Film 4-30 µm
  • Film thickness variation under 15%
  • Produces the HGR410 and HFR410 diamond grades

Supported processes

Graphite, composites, CFRPPCB ceramic substratesAlSi alloys (Si>12%)Woodworking toolsMicrocrystalline / Nanocrystalline diamond

Industries

Cutting and gear cutting tools, Aerospace and defence
