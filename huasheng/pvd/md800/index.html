MD800 arc coating machine

The MD800 PLUS is the arc coating machine at the heart of Huasheng's turnkey tool-coating plant. Low-voltage, high-current arc discharge evaporates and ionises the target material, which is deposited on the tools under an electric field, giving a high deposition rate and strong coating adhesion. Configured for high-throughput production of inserts, end mills, drills, and hob cutters.

Enquire about this View on Huasheng website

MD series overview

Arc ion plating range for cutting tool production, using Huasheng's lateral etching and multi arc ion plating. Four sizes from MD200 up to MD1500.

Typical applications

Inserts, end mills, drills. MD800 PLUS sits at the centre of the turnkey coating plant. MD1500 is the production machine for gear cutting tools - 72 hobbing tools and 1000 kg per batch.

Key features

  • High deposition rate
  • High ionisation rate
  • Ultra-high impact resistance
  • Fully automatic
  • MD800 PLUS and MD1500 are the only machines in the range that take hobbing tools (D80 x L150)

Supported processes

TiAlN, AlCrN and similar hard coatingsInserts, drills and end millsHobbing tools (D80 × L150)AlTiSiN and custom nano-composite coatings
