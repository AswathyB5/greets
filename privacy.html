DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) COMPLIANCE

Privacy Notice

Greets Equipment Pvt. Ltd. is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India).

Summary for Website Visitors: When you submit an equipment enquiry or contact request on this website, we collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, city, and component details strictly for responding to your technical enquiry and providing equipment proposals. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data Fiduciary Identity

Greets Equipment Pvt. Ltd. ("Greets", "we", "us", or "our") acts as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023 in respect of personal data collected through this website (greets.co.in).

Registered Office: HSR Layout, Bangalore, Karnataka 560102, India.
Official Contact Email: enquiry-equipment@greets.co.in

2. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data directly from you when you fill in our enquiry or contact form:

  • Contact Information: Name, business email address, phone/mobile number.
  • Professional Affiliation: Company/organization name, job role, and city/state location.
  • Technical Specifications: Details you provide regarding parts, substrate materials, production volumes, and heat treatment/cleaning/coating requirements.
  • Communication Records: Enquiries, responses, and correspondence records with our engineering team.

3. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the DPDP Act 2023, we process your personal data based on your explicit consent provided at the time of form submission, and for certain legitimate uses:

Technical Consultation

Evaluating your engineering requirements and recommending suitable BMI, Novatec, or Huasheng systems.

Quotation & Proposals

Preparing and delivering formal commercial quotes, machine layout proposals, and project schedules.

Support & Installation

Coordinating equipment delivery, on-site commissioning, operator training, and warranty support in India.

Legal Compliance

Maintaining necessary statutory records, commercial invoices, and tax compliance documentation.

4. Data Sharing and Cross-Border Transfers

As an authorised sales, installation, and service partner in India:

  • We may share specific technical part drawings or equipment inquiries with our OEM manufacturing partners: Fours Industriels B.M.I. (France), Novatec S.r.l. (Italy), and Guangdong Huasheng Nanotechnology (China), solely to produce accurate engineering simulations, cycle estimates, and machine configurations.
  • We ensure all OEM partners maintain confidentiality and robust data protection standards.
  • We never sell, rent, trade, or commercially monetize your personal data with third-party marketers.

5. Storage, Security and Retention

We implement technical, physical, and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

Data Retention: Equipment enquiry details and technical specifications are retained for up to 3 years to assist with engineering proposals and project discussions, unless you request earlier deletion.

6. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal in India, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request correction of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete data, or erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual who can exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent for future communications at any time.
