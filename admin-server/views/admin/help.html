How to use this admin panel

This panel lets you change the words, pictures, products and messages of the website yourself. You do not need to know anything about code. Read this once, it takes about five minutes.

The short version

  1. Choose what to change in the menu on the left.
  2. Click the words or the picture and change them.
  3. Press the green Save button. That is all: products go live on the website by themselves too.
  4. Refresh the website page to see it.
The menu Change words Bold, links and colours Pictures Add or delete items Products Enquiries If something goes wrong

1The menu on the left

DashboardA summary: how many enquiries and products you have, and the latest enquiries.
PagesThe pages of the website: Home page, Contact page, Privacy page and Header & Footer (the top menu and bottom of every page). Click a page to edit its words and pictures.
ProductsYour machines. Open BMI, Novatec or Huasheng, then a family, then a product. Click the word "Products" itself to see all three brands on one page.
EnquiriesThe messages visitors send from the contact form.
ActivityA list of what was changed and when.
View websiteOpens your website in a new tab.

Small arrows next to a name open and close its list. The ☰ button at the top left hides the whole menu to give you more room, and shows it again.

Finding your way back: every page has a ← Back button at the top, just above the heading. It takes you one step up, for example from a product to its family, or from a section to its page.

2Change the words of a page

  1. In the menu, click Home page (or Contact page, and so on).
  2. You see a list of sections. They are in the same order as on the website, from the top to the bottom. Click Edit next to the one you want. Each row shows a few of its words so you can recognise it.
  3. Click inside a box and type. Each box has a small name above it, such as "Sub-heading" or "Paragraph", so you know which part of the page it is. Once you change something, a green Edited tag appears.
  4. Press the green Save changes button at the bottom of the screen. It is always there, and it also appears inside each card.
  5. Open your website and refresh the page (Ctrl + Shift + R). The new words are there.
Made a mistake? Click Reset to original under a box. It brings back the very first words. Or press Discard to forget everything you have not saved yet.
Changes to pages are live as soon as you press Save. You do not need to do anything else.

3Make words bold, add a link or change the colour

Every text box works like a small word processor. You have to select the words first: press the mouse button at the start of the words, drag to the end and let go (or double-click one word). A dark toolbar then appears just above the words you selected:

Vacuum furnaces from France, supported in India
B BoldMakes the selected words thicker.
I ItalicMakes the selected words slanted.
LinkTurns the words into a link. A small box opens: type or paste where the link should go, then press Apply. You can write a web address (www.example.com), a page of your site (contact.html), an email address or a phone number. To take a link away, select the words again and press Remove link.
A ColourShows a few colours. Click one to colour the selected words.
Remove formattingTakes away bold, italic, colour and link from the selected words.

Press Enter to start a new line, just like in any editor. The toolbar goes away when you click somewhere else.

Not every box has this. Boxes for the address of a link, for the text inside an empty form field (a "placeholder") and for the choices of the contact-form dropdown stay plain, because they cannot show formatting on the website.

4Change a picture

  1. Open the section that has the picture. A small preview is shown next to its name.
  2. Press Upload new image and choose a file from your computer. It can be a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB.
  3. The preview changes at once. Press Save changes.
Use a picture with the same shape as the old one (wide stays wide, square stays square), so it fits the page nicely. Photos taken on a phone are often very large: that is fine up to 5 MB.

In Products a picture box has two buttons: Choose picks one of the pictures already on the website, and Upload adds a new one.

5Add or delete items in a list

Some parts of a page are lists of similar items: the people on the contact page, the industries, the choices in the contact-form dropdown. Each item is a card.

EditOpens or closes the card. All cards start closed so the page stays tidy.
DeleteRemoves the item from the website. It always asks "Delete ...?" first, so you cannot do it by accident.
RestoreAppears on a deleted item and brings it back.
+ Add new ...At the end of a list. Fill in the little form and press Add to website. The new item is a copy of the first one, with your words.
Where is the dropdown of the contact form? Pages → Contact page → Contact. The cards called "Dropdown choice" are the choices. Add or delete them there.

6Products

Products are arranged like folders: brand (BMI, Novatec, Huasheng) → family (for example "Gas quenching furnaces") → product (one machine). Use the menu on the left, or the pages in the middle of the screen.

Change a product

  1. Open Products, a brand, a family, and click the product (or its pencil).
  2. The form has numbered sections. On the right is a map of the real product page: click any part of the map to jump to the section that changes it. A box above it says "You are editing" and names the section and the box you are in.
  3. Under each box a grey line says "Shows as: ...": this is where those words or that picture appear on the product page.
  4. Press Save product (or the Save button inside any section).
Products go live by themselves. When you save, delete, reorder or switch a product to Draft, the website is updated a moment later. You do not press anything else. The bar at the top of the Products screens shows "Putting your change on the website..." for about a second, then "Live on the website". If something stops it (for example two products with exactly the same name) the bar turns red, says why, and has a Try again button. Your change is never lost: it is saved either way.

Other things you can do

7Enquiries from the contact form

When a visitor fills in the contact form on the website, the message appears under Enquiries, with the newest first.

  • New messages are shown in bold, marked New. Click Open to read one. It becomes Read.
  • After you answer, change the status to Replied, so everyone knows.
  • Use the search box to find a name, company or phone number.
  • Export to Excel (CSV) downloads the list as a file that opens in Excel. It follows the search and status filter you have chosen.

8If something goes wrong

I saved, but the website looks the sameRefresh the website page with Ctrl + Shift + R. Your browser may be showing an old copy. For products, look at the bar at the top: it says Live on the website, or tells you what is wrong.
I changed my mindBefore saving: press Discard. After saving: use Reset to original under the box.
I deleted an item by mistakeOn a page: press the Restore button on it. For a product, create it again (a deleted product cannot be restored).
A picture will not uploadUse a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF under 5 MB.
The page shows a red messageRead it: it says what to fix, and the box that needs attention is marked. Fix it and save again.
I cannot find a thing to editOnly what visitors can see on the website is here. Hidden technical things, such as the search-engine settings and the spam trap of the contact form, are left out on purpose.
I was signed outFor safety you are signed out after a few hours. Sign in again.

You can see who changed what, and when, under Activity.
