PLURITANK ultrasonic cleaning line

PLURITANK is Novatec's modular multi-chamber ultrasonic cleaning line. Process stations for ultrasonic cleaning, rinsing and drying are combined to suit the part, the contamination and the required cleanliness, with up to 14 process stations per line.

Enquire about this View on Novatec website

PLURITANK family overview

Modular multi-tank ultrasonic cleaning line. Built from separate process modules - ultrasonic immersion or spray cleaning, cascade rinsing, hot air and vacuum drying - so the line is configured to the job. Configurable tank volume from 50 to 1,500 litres with up to 14 process stations. Stainless steel throughout, PLC and HMI control, automatic basket handling from 10 kg to 300 kg and above.

Typical use

Cleaning tools and components before PVD coating and other vacuum treatments. Final cleaning before sterilisation and packing.

Key features

  • Modular, up to 14 process stations, with room for future hardware and software upgrades
