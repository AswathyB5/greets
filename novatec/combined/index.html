Combined turnkey implant lines

For larger projects Novatec designs the whole cleaning area to the customer's URS, linking the individual systems with conveyors and full automatic management. A typical line includes automatic basket loading with scanners and RFID, IPC one-chamber vacuum systems, quality verification stations, a combined final cleaning and passivation system, spray cleaning with an unload air lock, and a basket return conveyor, all with data exchange for traceability.
