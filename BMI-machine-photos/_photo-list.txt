IMPORTANT - THESE ARE NOT MODEL-SPECIFIC
BMI's presentation does not label its photographs with model numbers, so none
of these can be tied to a particular furnace model. Please use them as general
range and company images - category headers, the BMI brand page, backgrounds -
and do NOT caption any of them with a model name such as B85T or VSE84T.
The model data is in BMI-furnace-range-for-website.xlsx.
